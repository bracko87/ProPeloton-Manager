# Race Engine V3 — owner-facing milestone register

**Last updated: 2026-10-27, after the 1,280-run conditional time-slot adversarial study.** Research lives on GitHub `main` in isolated V3 files. The game's selected race engine and historical results have NOT been changed.

## Why this register exists

A numbered engineering batch is NOT evidence that the engine is nearing release. This register tracks outcomes, objective acceptance conditions and blockers. Do not replace a missing test with another numbered batch or silently claim percentage completion.

**A2 further rejected candidate (closing-wheel anticipation, 2026-10-26):** a conservative forecast of a slower wheel within two reaction ticks passed complete 106/168-rider simulations and exact checkpoint recovery, but did **not** improve classification stability: Dordrecht inversion share **7.619%→7.942%** (worse) and Guangxi **18.556%→18.556%** (unchanged). Original-grid gap and blocked-tick figures were identical; do not activate this option. [Detailed report](MILESTONE_A_03_EARLY_WHEEL_2026-10-26.md).

**Latest A2 micro result:** [Conditional corridor screening under delayed, reversing and multiple riders](MILESTONE_A_22_CONDITIONAL_ADVERSARIAL_2026-10-27.md) technically passed 1,280 scenarios with physical sweep, W-prime and checkpoint validation. It restores open-lane passing to 80/80 while protecting 64/64 early moving-blocker cases, but **late rival entry still loses 22/64 passes and causes 63 unrelated near-halted rider-ticks versus zero baseline**. In all 64 reversal cases the opponent actually moved and then reversed, but congestion remains. A2 sporting **NO-GO**, next current-intent-aware screening and sub-tick event order, not unapproved large-stage rollout.

**Immediate focus:** first full-stage technical gate is closed. The follow-up [A2 position-bias report](MILESTONE_A_02_START_GRID_2026-10-26.md) confirms fixed rider-ID priority **is not the cause**; coordinated overtaking capacity materially affects the result but does **not** pass sporting realism. Next: distinguish a geometrically nearby rival from an actively communicated lane-crossing intent, and verify safe response when the rival starts moving later. Log unresolved same-tick maneuver order, test multiple initial grids and protect both riders' complete movements before expanding beyond six riders. Source genuine widths/elevation and fix fractional finish energy independently. Do not consider gameplay integration ready on the strength of these research simulations.
